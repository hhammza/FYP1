Genomic Science
K-mer Analysis for Resistance Prediction
K-mer frequencies encode the compositional "fingerprint" of a genome resistance determinants leave distinctive patterns detectable by machine learning.
4-mers
256 Unique Tetranucleotides
A 4-mer (tetranucleotide) is any consecutive 4-nucleotide sequence (AAAA, AAAT, …, TTTT). With 4 bases, 4⁴ = 256 possible 4-mers exist. Their relative frequencies form a "spectrum" that is unique per organism and encodes genomic composition, codon usage, and repeat content.
RandomForest
Ensemble Decision Tree Classifier
RandomForest builds 100 independent decision trees on random subsets of features and training data, then votes for the majority class. class_weight='balanced' corrects for class imbalance. This ensemble approach achieves AUC-ROC 0.9290 with robustness to outlier genomes.
Resistance Genes
Genomic Determinants
Resistance genes alter k-mer frequencies around their insertion sites. Beta-lactamase genes (blaTEM, blaSHV, blaKPC), efflux pumps (acrAB-tolC, mexAB-oprM), and aminoglycoside-modifying enzymes each leave detectable composition signatures in the 4-mer spectrum.
