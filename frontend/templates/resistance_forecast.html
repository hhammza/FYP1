Scientific Background
Understanding LightGBM-Based AMR Forecasting
The model uses gradient boosting on {{ metrics.lgbm.train_rows }} labelled records to classify antibiotic resistance.
LightGBM
Gradient Boosting Algorithm
LightGBM builds an ensemble of decision trees sequentially, each correcting the errors of the previous. It uses histogram-based splitting and leaf-wise tree growth for superior speed and accuracy on tabular genomic metadata. On genomes it never saw in training it scores AUC-ROC {{ metrics.lgbm.auc_ci }}.
MIC Values
Minimum Inhibitory Concentration
MIC is the lowest antibiotic concentration that prevents visible bacterial growth. It is the gold-standard phenotypic measure of resistance. Our model log-transforms MIC values and encodes the sign (≤, =, ≥) as a separate categorical feature, capturing both measurement and directionality.
14 Features
Genomic Feature Engineering
The model uses 14 engineered features: antibiotic name, drug class, MIC value + sign, genus, species, taxon ID, and target-encoded resistance rates per antibiotic, per taxon-antibiotic pair, and per genus-antibiotic pair, capturing population-level resistance patterns.

Model Training Pipeline
1Load every AMR CSV file from the BVBRC/PATRIC export ({{ metrics.lgbm.train_rows }} training rows)
2Parse MIC values, extract genus/species, merge Intermediate→Resistant
3Target-encode resistance rates per antibiotic, taxon, genus (5-fold CV)
4Train LightGBM with is_unbalance=True and early stopping (50 rounds)
5Evaluate on genomes held out of training with AUC-ROC: {{ metrics.lgbm.auc_ci }}

Antibiotic Drug Classes: Mechanism of Action
Beta-Lactams
Inhibit cell wall synthesis by binding penicillin-binding proteins (PBPs). Include penicillins, cephalosporins, carbapenems. Resistance via beta-lactamase enzymes (ESBL, KPC).
Fluoroquinolones
Inhibit DNA gyrase and topoisomerase IV, blocking DNA replication. Include ciprofloxacin, levofloxacin. Resistance via target mutations and efflux pumps (QRDR mutations).
Aminoglycosides
Bind 30S ribosomal subunit, inhibiting protein synthesis. Include gentamicin, tobramycin, amikacin. Resistance via aminoglycoside-modifying enzymes (AMEs) and 16S rRNA methylases.
Tetracyclines
Inhibit 30S ribosomal subunit, blocking aminoacyl-tRNA binding. Include tetracycline, doxycycline, tigecycline. Resistance via efflux pumps (tet genes) and ribosomal protection proteins.
