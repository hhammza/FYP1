Bottom line. The deployed models are weaker mainly because of their data, not their algorithm: they learned from {{ p(lgp.rows / full.rows) }} and {{ p(kmp.genomes / km.genomes_with_fasta) }} of what was available, from a narrow set of bacteria, and were scored on genomes they had already seen.
