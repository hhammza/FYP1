Trend Forecast

Mutation Timeline

A trained model that forecasts, year by year, how much of an organism resists a drug.

Resistance Trend Forecast
LightGBM · BV-BRC lab results with a collection year
ML model

BV-BRC laboratory results with a collection year are grouped per genus and antibiotic into a yearly share of resistant isolates. From the years before it only (the last values, the recent slope, the gap since the last value, the genus and the drug class), LightGBM predicts how the share changes next year, and repeats that for up to ten years with an 80% band.

Choose the organism, or upload a genome and its genus is identified from its 6-mer profile. Trained on years up to 2016 and tested on 2017 to 2022, it beats a straight-line trend and the historical average, and edges out repeating last year's value. A second tab keeps the week-by-week what-if simulation, labelled as not a trained model, with the RL agent's treatment plan.

Methodology

How the System Works

Five trained ML models (LightGBM ×4 + an RL agent) and 1 simulation: the models predict resistance now and forecast how it changes year by year; the what-if simulation shows how it might evolve week by week.

01
Data Collection: BVBRC/PATRIC
AMR phenotype records (Resistant/Susceptible/Intermediate) linked to bacterial genome metadata including MIC values, laboratory methods, and FASTA genome sequences from the BVBRC database.
02
Feature Engineering: K-mer Spectra
Bacterial genomes are converted to 4-mer frequency vectors (256 dimensions). Each genome produces a fixed-length fingerprint capturing codon composition, GC content, and repeat patterns. No reference genome is needed.
03
LightGBM: Tabular Metadata
For records without FASTA files, a LightGBM gradient boosted tree model uses tabular features: MIC value, antibiotic identity, drug class, organism taxonomy, and cross-encoded resistance rates per antibiotic.
04
Class Imbalance Correction
Training data is typically imbalanced (more Susceptible than Resistant isolates). Both models are LightGBM with is_unbalance=True, and each one's decision threshold is set on validation genomes to miss at most 10% of resistant isolates, without synthetic oversampling.
05
Timeline: Yearly Trend Forecast
Lab results with a collection year become a yearly resistant share per genus and antibiotic. A LightGBM model learns how that share moves from one year to the next, and forecasts up to ten years ahead with an 80% band.
06
Validation: AUC-ROC & F1
Both models are tested on genomes they never saw in training: the forecaster scores AUC-ROC {{ metrics.lgbm.auc_ci }} and the k-mer model {{ metrics.kmer.auc_ci }}. The forecaster's threshold ({{ metrics.lgbm.threshold }}) is chosen on validation genomes to keep missed resistance (very major error) at or below 10%, because calling a resistant isolate susceptible is the dangerous mistake.
Get Started

Quick Start Guide

01
Train Models (optional)

Models are pre-trained. To retrain on your dataset, go to Train Models or run python train_models.py --model all in the backend folder.

02
AMR Forecasting

Go to AMR Forecasting and enter an antibiotic name, MIC value, and organism genus/species. The LightGBM model returns resistance probability instantly.

03
Genome-level Analysis

Upload a .fasta file to Resistance Prediction for k-mer analysis, or to Mutation Timeline for a week-by-week evolution forecast.
